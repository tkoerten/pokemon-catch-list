Quick IV reminder

Great & Ultra: perfect PvP IVs are often lower Attack with high Defense and HP—not always 15/15/15.

Master: there is no CP cap, so look for 15/15/15 or near-perfect IVs and save XL Candy.

Rankings use PvPoke Open League. Availability can change—check in-game before making plans.
